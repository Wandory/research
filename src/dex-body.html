Method

Fee share is the fee rate divided by the sum of fee and reward rate, weighted by pool size. A pool enters only when the fee rate is non null; on those pools a missing reward rate counts as zero. Emissions in the baseline are the reward rate divided by 36,500, multiplied by pool size, summed over days. The live aggregate applies a floor of one hundred thousand dollars per pool; the explorer below lets that floor be moved.

Sources

yields.llama.fi/pools, fetched by this page at open time. Baseline panel from the study published September 2026, covering 143 pools and 78,412 pool days across six venues.

Limits

Impermanent loss is not accounted for anywhere here. The fee rate in the source is fees over TVL and excludes any change in the value of the position, so this page describes what the advertised yield is made of and not whether a liquidity provider made money. Vote-escrow emissions are not decomposed: at Curve, Aerodrome and Velodrome part of the payout is bought by third parties and still appears as a reward from the venue. Venues whose pools currently publish no fee rate show as no live data rather than zero.
