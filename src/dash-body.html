Live protocol economics

Rates and deposits come from the yields endpoint, fees and revenue from the fees endpoint, both read directly by your browser. Subsidy run rate annualises today's reward rate, so it is a forward projection and not comparable with realised spend. Dependency is that run rate over annualised revenue; above one means the protocol hands out more than it earns. Click any heading to sort.

Method

Share of yield paid by borrowers is the base rate divided by the sum of base and reward rate, weighted by pool size. A pool enters only when the base rate is non null; on those pools a missing reward rate counts as zero. Subsidies are the reward rate divided by 36,500, multiplied by pool size, summed over days; the live figure annualises the current day instead. Protocol scopes are matched between the yields and fees endpoints, so Aave v3 is read as aave-v3 and not as the Aave parent.

Sources

yields.llama.fi/pools and api.llama.fi/summary/fees/{slug}, fetched by this page at open time. Baseline panel from the study published September 2026, which covers January 2024 to August 2026 across 478 pools and 146,866 pool days.

Limits

The live column is a snapshot of one moment and is noisier than the monthly panel. Reward rates are as published by the protocol and priced at the aggregator's token price, and announced rewards cannot be told apart from paid ones. Borrower side rewards are not in supply pool rates, so real subsidies are higher than shown, most of all at Moonwell and Venus.
