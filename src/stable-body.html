Method

Realised yield is computed by log-compounding daily rates and annualising, never by averaging them. Windows are stepped every 15 days and discarded when a gap in the series exceeds a quarter of the window length. Only windows whose entry rate was at least 3% a year enter the ratio, that is, the ones a holder would plausibly act on. The live rate is the base rate weighted by pool size across the issuer's pools.

Sources

yields.llama.fi/pools, fetched by this page at open time. Baseline panel from the study published September 2026, covering 38 pools across eight issuers.

Limits

Fees and slippage are excluded: the yield here is the product's rate, not the investor's bottom line. Risk is not measured, so depegs, collateral quality and counterparty risk are invisible in this data. History lengths differ between issuers, which is why annual windows do not exist for all of them. Rates come from one aggregator with no reconciliation against issuer contracts.
