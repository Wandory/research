Method

All V1 vaults holding at least one million dollars are taken. Curator names come from the curators query and are matched to each vault through the curator address on its state; a vault whose address matches no curator is shown as no curator listed. Rate is netApy, the rate a depositor actually receives after the fee. Collateral shares are computed per allocation, weighted by the amount allocated, and blue chip means WETH, wstETH, cbETH, rETH, weETH, ezETH, WBTC, cbBTC, tBTC, LBTC, USDC, USDT, DAI or USDS.

Sources

blue-api.morpho.org/graphql, queries vaults and curators, fetched by this page at open time. Baseline snapshot from the study published September 2026, covering 78 vaults and a year of daily history for the thirty largest.

Limits

Six vaults are excluded for quoting a rate above 100% a year or a fee above 35%. They are unlisted vaults whose share price has been pushed to hundreds of dollars in a vault denominated in a dollar asset, which is what the quoted rate is derived from; none of the listed vaults in the sample is affected. This is a snapshot rather than a daily series, so the live column moves with the market and is noisier than a monthly panel would be. V2 vaults are out of scope, which is why the total does not match protocol TVL at aggregators, where collateral held in isolated markets is also counted. A curator can run vaults under more than one address, and any address the curators query does not list falls into the unlisted row.
